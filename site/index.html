Alps

A simple and extensible webmail.

Free and open source · MIT License

Alps is a webmail you run beside your own mail server. It signs in to IMAP and SMTP with each user’s own credentials and keeps no database: every account’s settings live on the mail server, and the whole web interface ships inside a single binary.

  • Mail, conversations and search
  • Calendars, tasks and contacts
  • Filters and auto-reply
  • OpenPGP in the browser
  • Security keys for sign-in
  • 19 languages, right-to-left too
